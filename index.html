Geo Commodities Publisher

Geo Commodities Publisher is a private automation tool operated by Minh Hai. It prepares short news videos about how geopolitical events relate to commodity prices and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel, [CHANNEL NAME], after the operator approves each video.
