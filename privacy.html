Geo Commodities Publisher (the app) is a private tool operated by Minh Hai (minhhai.110494@gmail.com). It has a single user: the owner of the YouTube channel Geo Commodities.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload), the app uploads videos and thumbnails, with titles, descriptions and tags supplied by the owner, to the owner's own channel. The app does not read, collect or store personal data about any other YouTube user, viewer or channel.

Storage, sharing and deletion

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer or server. It is never shared, sold or disclosed to third parties. The only data kept are the video files and metadata created by the owner. To delete the stored token, the owner deletes the file token.json. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (also available at myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation the app can no longer access the channel.
